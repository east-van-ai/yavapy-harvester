## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-05

First release.

- Four commands write the CSV: `walkdown` from PyPI's newest change down, `catchup`
  up from its mark, `backfill` through a list of names, and `update` for one project.
- The CSV holds one row per console project, with or without a skill.
- Skills are counted at the release tag, `v{version}` or `{version}`.
